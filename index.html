The eternal cycle

Three virtues. One legend.

Across shifting ages and different kingdoms, the series returns to a timeless tension between courage, wisdom, and power — and to the people who choose what to do with them.

I

Courage

To step beyond the familiar, enter the unknown, and keep moving when the path ahead is hidden.

II

Wisdom

To understand the world, solve what seems impossible, and see the answer where others see a wall.

III

Power

A force that can protect, conquer, restore, or corrupt — depending entirely on the hand that holds it.

1986 → 2024

Launch timeline & story guide.

Twenty-one core original releases, arranged by first launch year. Open any entry for a concise, spoiler-light synopsis of the adventure and its original platform.

This guide focuses on original core Zelda adventures. Remakes, remasters, ports, and spin-offs are intentionally excluded.

21 adventures
  1. 1986
    The Legend of ZeldaThe first questFamicom Disk System / NES＋
    Story synopsis

    Hyrule has fallen into chaos after Ganon seizes the Triforce of Power. Before her capture, Princess Zelda divides the Triforce of Wisdom into eight fragments and hides them across the kingdom. A young traveler named Link sets out to recover the pieces, brave the land’s dungeons, and challenge the evil holding Hyrule in its grip.

  2. 1987
    Zelda II: The Adventure of LinkThe sleeping princessFamicom Disk System / NES＋
    Story synopsis

    A mysterious crest appears on Link’s hand and leads him to the legend of another Princess Zelda, trapped in an enchanted sleep. To awaken her, Link must restore crystals to six distant palaces and prove himself worthy of the Triforce of Courage while Ganon’s followers hunt him in hopes of reviving their fallen master.

  3. 1991
    A Link to the PastLight and Dark WorldsSuper Famicom / SNES＋
    Story synopsis

    When the sorcerer Agahnim takes control of Hyrule Castle and begins sending the descendants of the Seven Sages into another realm, Link is drawn into an ancient struggle. His search for the Master Sword carries him between Hyrule’s Light World and a corrupted Dark World, where the true force behind the kingdom’s crisis waits.

  4. 1993
    Link’s AwakeningThe dreamlike islandGame Boy＋
    Story synopsis

    After a storm destroys his ship, Link awakens on the mysterious Koholint Island, a place filled with odd characters, impossible creatures, and secrets that feel half remembered. The only path home is said to be through the Wind Fish, so Link searches the island’s dungeons for eight musical instruments needed to awaken the sleeping being.

  5. 1998
    Ocarina of TimeThe Hero of TimeNintendo 64＋
    Story synopsis

    A boy raised in Kokiri Forest is summoned into a conflict that will decide the fate of Hyrule. Link joins Princess Zelda in an attempt to stop Ganondorf from reaching the Sacred Realm, but destiny carries him seven years into a devastated future. As both child and adult, he awakens the Sages and fights to repair the damage caused by Ganondorf’s rise.

  6. 2000
    Majora’s MaskThree days remainNintendo 64＋
    Story synopsis

    Searching for a lost friend, Link is drawn into the parallel land of Termina, where a sinister mask has set the moon on a collision course with the world. With only three repeating days before disaster, Link uses the Ocarina of Time and a collection of transformative masks to help Termina’s people and uncover the tragedy behind Majora’s power.

  7. 2001
    Oracle of AgesAges intertwinedGame Boy Color＋
    Story synopsis

    The Triforce sends Link to Labrynna, where the Oracle of Ages, Nayru, is possessed by the sorceress Veran. History begins to unravel as Veran manipulates the past to reshape the present. Link travels between the two eras, restoring the flow of time and gathering the strength needed to confront the forces working behind the crisis.

  8. 2001
    Oracle of SeasonsThe seasons in chaosGame Boy Color＋
    Story synopsis

    Link is transported to Holodrum just as the armored General Onox kidnaps Din, the Oracle of Seasons. With nature thrown out of balance, Link learns to command spring, summer, autumn, and winter using the Rod of Seasons. His journey crosses a transformed landscape and connects with Oracle of Ages to reveal a larger plot.

  9. 2002
    Four SwordsFour heroes, one bladeGame Boy Advance＋
    Story synopsis

    The wind mage Vaati escapes confinement and abducts Princess Zelda, forcing Link to draw the legendary Four Sword. The blade divides him into four heroes who must work together through a series of shifting stages, gather rupees, and reach Vaati’s palace. The adventure introduced cooperative multiplayer as a central part of Zelda’s design.

  10. 2002
    The Wind WakerThe Great SeaNintendo GameCube＋
    Story synopsis

    On a small island in a vast ocean, Link’s quiet life changes when a giant bird kidnaps his sister, Aryll. His rescue attempt brings him aboard the pirate Tetra’s ship and into a forgotten conflict beneath the Great Sea. Sailing from island to island, Link uncovers the fate of old Hyrule and the return of an ancient enemy.

  11. 2004
    Four Swords AdventuresHyrule dividedNintendo GameCube＋
    Story synopsis

    When dark clouds gather over Hyrule and a mysterious Shadow Link appears, Princess Zelda and the shrine maidens are taken. Link draws the Four Sword and is once again divided into four. Their quest leads through a Hyrule filled with traps, armies, and old magic as the heroes discover that Vaati is not the only danger moving behind the scenes.

  12. 2004
    The Minish CapA tiny worldGame Boy Advance＋
    Story synopsis

    During Hyrule’s Picori Festival, the sorcerer Vaati shatters the sacred Picori Blade and turns Princess Zelda to stone. Link teams up with Ezlo, a strange talking cap who can shrink him to Minish size. Moving between the human world and tiny hidden communities, Link works to restore the blade and stop Vaati from claiming a legendary power.

  13. 2006
    Twilight PrincessA kingdom in shadowWii / Nintendo GameCube＋
    Story synopsis

    A spreading curtain of Twilight begins consuming Hyrule, transforming Link into a wolf whenever he enters the shadowed realm. With the mysterious Midna as his guide, he restores light to the kingdom piece by piece. Their journey exposes the ambitions of the usurper Zant and a deeper threat tied to Hyrule’s oldest struggle for power.

  14. 2007
    Phantom HourglassThe Ghost ShipNintendo DS＋
    Story synopsis

    After the events of The Wind Waker, Tetra disappears while investigating the legendary Ghost Ship. Link gives chase and washes up in an unfamiliar sea, where he meets the sailor Linebeck and the enigmatic Oshus. The search for Tetra becomes a voyage across new islands and repeated descents into the dangerous Temple of the Ocean King.

  15. 2009
    Spirit TracksRails across New HyruleNintendo DS＋
    Story synopsis

    Generations after a new Hyrule is founded, its sacred railway network begins disappearing and Princess Zelda’s body is stolen during a plot to revive an ancient demon king. Zelda’s spirit survives and joins a young engineer named Link. Together they restore the Spirit Tracks, explore the Tower of Spirits, and race to prevent the kingdom’s seals from collapsing.

  16. 2011
    Skyward SwordBefore the kingdomWii＋
    Story synopsis

    Long before the familiar kingdom of Hyrule, Link lives above the clouds on the floating island of Skyloft. When Zelda is swept to the surface below, he follows with the guidance of Fi and the Goddess Sword. The journey crosses ancient ruins, repeating eras, and a primordial war whose consequences echo through the legends that follow.

  17. 2013
    A Link Between WorldsTwo kingdomsNintendo 3DS＋
    Story synopsis

    A sorcerer named Yuga begins turning Hyrule’s Sages into paintings and escaping through cracks between worlds. Link gains the unusual ability to become a painting himself, letting him slip along walls and cross into the parallel kingdom of Lorule. There, a second royal family and a dying world complicate what first seemed like a familiar battle.

  18. 2015
    Tri Force HeroesA co-op fairy taleNintendo 3DS＋
    Story synopsis

    In the fashion-obsessed kingdom of Hytopia, Princess Styla is cursed into an outfit she cannot remove. Three heroes who share the legendary pointed ears answer the kingdom’s call and travel into the Drablands. By stacking into a Totem and coordinating items and costumes, the trio works through elaborate stages to break the witch’s curse.

  19. 2017
    Breath of the WildA ruined HyruleWii U / Nintendo Switch＋
    Story synopsis

    Link awakens after a century with almost no memory of the war that destroyed Hyrule. Calamity Ganon remains sealed inside Hyrule Castle while Princess Zelda holds the disaster at bay. Free to travel almost anywhere, Link rebuilds his strength, recovers memories of the fallen Champions, and prepares to face the calamity that defeated the kingdom one hundred years earlier.

  20. 2023
    Tears of the KingdomSky, surface, and DepthsNintendo Switch＋
    Story synopsis

    An expedition beneath Hyrule Castle awakens Ganondorf and triggers the Upheaval, scattering ruins into the sky and opening vast chasms across the land. Zelda disappears, Link loses the Master Sword, and a mysterious new arm grants him strange abilities. His search spans Hyrule’s surface, floating islands, and underground Depths while revealing a story rooted in the kingdom’s distant past.

  21. 2024
    Echoes of WisdomThe princess takes the leadNintendo Switch＋
    Story synopsis

    After Link vanishes into a mysterious rift, strange tears begin swallowing people and places throughout Hyrule. Princess Zelda escapes captivity and joins an ethereal companion named Tri, whose Tri Rod can create echoes of objects and monsters. Armed with ingenuity rather than a traditional hero’s toolkit, Zelda enters the Still World to mend the rifts and save her kingdom.

A fan's shortcut through history

Four eras worth revisiting.

This is not an official canon timeline — just a clean way to browse a few major phases of the series.

1986 → 1993

The foundation

The original adventure, Zelda II, A Link to the Past, and Link's Awakening established exploration, items, dungeons, and mystery.

1998 → 2006

The 3D revolution

Ocarina of Time, Majora's Mask, The Wind Waker, and Twilight Princess reimagined the series in sweeping new forms.

2007 → 2015

Experimentation

Portable adventures, motion controls, remakes, and A Link Between Worlds kept reshaping what Zelda could feel like.

2017 → today

Open-air Hyrule

Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom pushed systemic play, freedom, verticality, and player creativity to the forefront.
