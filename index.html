The eternal cycle

Three virtues. One legend.

Across shifting ages and different kingdoms, the series returns to a timeless tension between courage, wisdom, and power — and to the people who choose what to do with them.

I

Courage

To step beyond the familiar, enter the unknown, and keep moving when the path ahead is hidden.

II

Wisdom

To understand the world, solve what seems impossible, and see the answer where others see a wall.

III

Power

A force that can protect, conquer, restore, or corrupt — depending entirely on the hand that holds it.

A fan's shortcut through history

Four eras worth revisiting.

This is not an official canon timeline — just a clean way to browse a few major phases of the series.

1986 → 1993

The foundation

The original adventure, Zelda II, A Link to the Past, and Link's Awakening established exploration, items, dungeons, and mystery.

1998 → 2006

The 3D revolution

Ocarina of Time, Majora's Mask, The Wind Waker, and Twilight Princess reimagined the series in sweeping new forms.

2007 → 2015

Experimentation

Portable adventures, motion controls, remakes, and A Link Between Worlds kept reshaping what Zelda could feel like.

2017 → today

Open-air Hyrule

Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom pushed systemic play, freedom, verticality, and player creativity to the forefront.
